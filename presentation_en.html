⚡ Two Hazards Arriving Together
  • Urban Heat Island & Drought: Summer heat stresses crops and spikes urban discomfort index.

05 — CONTROL LOGIC & ALGORITHM
5-Second Decision Algorithm Flowchart
TEAM ACE · SDS PLATFORM
[ System Decision Algorithm Flowchart ]
Six Operating Modes
Operating Mode Condition & Control Action
FLOOD_PREVENTION P2 Pre-drain Pump active (empties tank to ≤ 10% level)
GREENERY_IRRIGATION P1 Water Pump 12 s pulse ON (~0.34 L micro-dosing)
IRRIGATION_SOAK Waiting 10 min for soil absorption before re-evaluating
LOW_WATER_WARNING Hot & dry, but tank water storage level ≤ 20%
STANDBY Normal monitoring mode (holds harvested rainwater)
FAIL_SAFE Sensor / API outage — all relay pumps shut OFF for safety
* Priority is Strict: Heavy rain forecast (flood prevention) takes absolute priority. Rain forecast arriving mid-irrigation instantly halts P1 and triggers P2 pre-drainage.

06 — HARDWARE RELIABILITY
Engineering Solutions for Real Hardware
TEAM ACE · SDS PLATFORM
Six reliability solutions engineered to resolve sensor noise, deadband chatter, and communication dropouts in physical hardware.
Concern As Specified ACE Implemented Solution Improvement / Why
Threshold Chatter Irrigate below 30% moisture Deadband hysteresis (dry <28%, recovered >35%) Prevents rapid pump ON/OFF toggling near boundary readings
Over-irrigation Control Pump ON while conditions hold 12 s pulse (~0.34 L) + 10 min soak hold Prevents draining full tank while sensor slowly reacts
Pre-drain Stop Open above 20% Opens at 20%, closes at 10%, 300 s safety cap Prevents pump dry-running and infinite drain loops
Bad Data / Outage — Stale >30 s or missing telemetry ➔ FAIL_SAFE (all OFF) A silent or failed sensor never misreads as "dry"
💡 Design Principle: All policy logic lives on the Raspberry Pi software (modifiable without reflashing), while the ESP32 maintains local hardware guardrails if connection drops.

💡 Design Upgrade: Solenoid Valve ➔ Second Submersible Pump
A 5 V solenoid valve requires ≈0.02 MPa to open. The 200 mm tank head provides 0.002 MPa — 10x too low. Replacing with a second submersible pump eliminates pressure requirements and ensures 100% reliable drainage.
