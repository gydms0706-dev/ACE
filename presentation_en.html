01 — BACKGROUND & PROBLEM
Extreme Summer Climate Hazards in Urban Environments
TEAM ACE · SDS PLATFORM
Modern cities face compound climate hazards from alternating heatwaves and flash floods.
1. Heatwave & Drought
• Extreme heat dries soil below 30% moisture.
• Heat island effect causes plant wilting & discomfort.
• Manual watering wastes municipal tap water.
2. Torrential Rain & Flooding
• Downpours exceed storm sewer capacity.
• Rainwater tanks fill to 100% and overflow into streets.
• Lack of pre-storm drainage leads to urban flooding.
3. Limits of Conventional Systems
• Existing drains operate as one-way reactive systems.
• Stormwater is dumped into sewers instead of stored.
• No integration between flood prevention & irrigation.
The Need for an Integrated Solution
Urban infrastructure requires a smart system capable of automatically switching between storing rainwater for drought irrigation and pre-draining tanks before severe storms land.

02 — CORE CONCEPT
One Tank, Two Jobs: Bi-Directional Climate Management
TEAM ACE · SDS PLATFORM
JOB 1: Heatwave Micro-Irrigation (P1 Pump)
  • Trigger Condition: Clear weather, Soil moisture < 30%, Temp ≥ 30°C, and Tank storage > 20%.
  • Control Action: Activates P1 Irrigation Pump to deliver a 12-second pulse (~0.34 L) to the crop box.
  • Benefit: Recycles stored rainwater to preserve urban crops without wasting tap water.
JOB 2: Pre-Storm Flood Drainage (P2 Pump)
  • Trigger Condition: KMA Open API receives Heavy Rain Forecast AND Tank storage level > 20%.
  • Control Action: Activates P2 Pre-drain Pump to empty the tank down to 10% before downpour hits.
  • Benefit: Creates an empty storage buffer in advance, preventing urban sewer overload.
The Rainwater Circularity Loop
The torrential downpour that threatens street flooding is stored into the tank, becoming the exact water source that cools the city during the next heatwave.

03 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
System Architecture & Communication Protocol
TEAM ACE · SDS PLATFORM
1. ESP32 Edge Node & Local Hardware
• Sensors: Capacitive Soil Moisture (ADC1), DHT22 Temp/Humidity, HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Tank Level.
• Actuators: 2-Channel 5V Relay controlling P1 Irrigation Pump & P2 Pre-drainage Pump.
• Execution Role: Reads telemetry every 2 s, reports via MQTT JSON, executes commands with 15 s safety watchdog.
2. KMA Weather Forecast Open API
• Open API Polling: Fetches short-term weather forecast every 10 min from KMA data portal.
• Grid Conversion: Converts lat/lon geographic coordinates to Lambert conformal conic grid.
• Fail-Safe: API outages default to "last valid forecast" then "no rain", preventing erroneous drainage.
Telemetry Payload (ESP32 → Pi, 2 s)
{ "ts": 1786342072.4, "node": "ace-esp32", "soil_moisture_pct": 27.4, "ambient_temp_c": 34.2, "tank_level_pct": 43.1 }
Command Payload (Pi → ESP32, 5 s)
{ "mode": "GREENERY_IRRIGATION", "pump": true, "drain": false, "reason": "moisture 27%, 34.2C", "ttl_s": 15.0 }

04 — CONTROL LOGIC & ALGORITHM
System Decision Logic
TEAM ACE · SDS PLATFORM
Core Decision Engine

5-Second Real-Time Decision Algorithm

Evaluates sensor data and weather forecast every 5 seconds to automatically toggle between pre-storm flood protection and heatwave micro-irrigation.

⏱ Real-Time Sensor & Weather Polling
Polls KMA forecast API, soil moisture, ambient temp, and water tank storage level every 5-second cycle.
🛡 Heavy Rain Priority Override
Heavy rain forecast triggers instant pre-drainage via P2 pump, clearing tank storage capacity before storm hits.
🌱 Smart Micro-Irrigation
Doses a 0.34 L pulse via P1 pump during hot (≥30°C) and dry (<30% moisture) conditions when stored water is available.
